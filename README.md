# 🍔 ALL THE NOMS!

A foodie social network for people who love their favorite dishes and want to collect them all!

## 📋 Overview

ALL THE NOMS! is a vibrant foodie social network built around your favorite dishes. We understand that special feeling when you go to a restaurant and say "I'm gonna have my usual." Whether you're looking to find new places that serve your beloved dish, earn bragging rights by trying every version in town, or scientifically rank them all - ALL THE NOMS! is your culinary companion.

Our platform helps food enthusiasts:
- Track and collect versions of their favorite dishes across different restaurants
- Share photos and detailed reviews of their culinary experiences
- Connect with other foodies who share their taste preferences
- Discover new restaurants based on personalized recommendations
- Compete in friendly challenges to become the ultimate dish connoisseur

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Create an engaging, accessible platform for food enthusiasts across all devices
- Build a robust recommendation engine based on shared taste preferences
- Develop an intuitive taxonomy system for categorizing diverse food items
- Implement gamification features to encourage exploration and sharing
- Support offline functionality for on-the-go food journeys
- Foster authentic foodie communities around specific dishes
